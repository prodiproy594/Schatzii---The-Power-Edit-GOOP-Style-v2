Moving between city desks, ride-shares, and airport terminals often turns into a search for wall outlets. The secret to modern mobile focus isn't carrying more battery capacity—it’s carrying versatile devices that recharge automatically.

The Strategy: Keep the T-Bone docked in your car console so it is always full before you step out, pack the razor-thin Powerstick into your notebook sleeve, and keep the Wally Dual USB Wall Charger plugged into your hotel nightstand. Minimal friction, zero dead batteries.
